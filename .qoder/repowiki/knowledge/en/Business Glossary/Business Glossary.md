### AOTMS Global
- Definition：Short form of AOTMS Global Pvt. Ltd., the company whose software products are marketed on this landing page.
- Aliases：AOTMS

### LMS
- Definition：Learning Management System — one of AOTMS's six core products, covering learning/course management, student/trainer management, assessments and progress tracking.

### CRM
- Definition：Customer Relationship Management — one of AOTMS's six core products, covering lead management, employee/task management, attendance/performance tracking, and business operations management.

### WhatsApp Automation
- Definition：One of AOTMS's six core products providing automated messaging, customer communication, WhatsApp chatbot/AI agent, and automated follow-ups.

### AI Calling
- Definition：One of AOTMS's six core products providing AI-powered inbound/outbound calling, automated lead conversations, lead qualification, and call management/reporting.

### PitchBid
- Definition：The branded name of AOTMS's Auction Platform product, handling player/team auction management, live bidding, and auction management across sports.
- Aliases：Auction Platform

### ERP Solutions
- Definition：One of AOTMS's six core products covering business process management, inventory management, finance and operational management.

### Get a Demo
- Definition：Primary conversion CTA used throughout the landing page (navbar, hero, CTA section, footer); its behaviour — scroll to contact block, open an on-page form/modal, or link out externally — is still to be decided.
- Aliases：Request a Demo、Talk to Our Team
